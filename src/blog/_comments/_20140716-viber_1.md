This also works great for [pipelight](http://pipelight.net) which allows you to use many windows plugins in firefox.
I tried it with silverlight (for Netflix), but have heard it also works for flash, widewine etc.

To get Silverlight working, follow the instructions [here](http://pipelight.net/cms/install/installation-debian.html) in your `chroot`.
Be sure to also [enable sliverlight](http://pipelight.net/cms/installation.html#section_2) and [change your user agent](http://pipelight.net/cms/installation-user-agent.html).
As of 2014-08-09 Netflix works perfectly with `iceweasel-30.0-2`.
