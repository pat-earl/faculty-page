Research Interests

{% markdown %} My research concerns **"Theoretical Applied Math"** using tools from partial differential equations and probability. More specifically, I've worked on a variety of problems related to anomalous diffusion, coagulation, fluid mechanics, homogenization, Q-tensors and mixing. {% endmarkdown %}
