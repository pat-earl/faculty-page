## Acceptable Use Policy

**Due:**
: Monday, June 27 by 11:59PM

**Purpose:**
: Defining what is acceptable use of university owned IT equipment. 


## Description

As a system administrator for a small private university, you've been tasked with 
creating an acceptable use policy for faculty, staff, and students who use the IT 
equipment owned by the university. Write your policy keeping in mind the unique
needs of a university when it comes to IT, compared to say a financial company. 

Your policy should be between 1 and 1.5 pages in length, using a 12-point Times font, single spaced. You should include at least a paragraph or two justifying your policy and explaining
your point of view. 

You may refer to [Kutztown's Acceptable Use Policy](https://apps.kutztown.edu/SZ_001_POLICY_REGISTER/policy.aspx?policy=ACA-069) and other universities to help
come up with your own. Be sure to include a *Works Cited* page in your document
for any you use, *including* Kutztown's.

## Submission

Submit as a Word document or PDF file on D2L under the assignment titled 
*Acceptable Use Policy*. No other formats will be accepted.
Your name, date, and assignment number should be included in some form at the top.
