## Details

For the final project you are going to find a dataset and visualize it using Tableau, Excel, or
whatever software you'd like. Your visualizations will summarize findings and patterns within the
dataset. At a minimum you'll need to create two visualizations.

The following places are recommended to search for datasets, but feel free to expand your search if needed:

* [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com)
* [USA Data](https://www.data.gov)
* [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/)

Take note that you should be searching for data sets, **not** visualizations. Kaggle and Data.gov
are the best to place to find free datasets, while Google's search may return ones that require payment. 
Refer back to the readings on *Tidy-Data* when searching for datasets. 

Once you find your data, generate some questions relating to it. These questions will be used to 
produce your visualizations at a later time. 

Please send me an email about your dataset by the date specified above for **Dataset Selection**. 
In your email include the following:

* A link to where you found your dataset. 
* Short description of the dataset and how you plan to visualize it. 
* What software you'll be using (Tableau, Excel, etc.)

Failure to submit this information by the due date will result in an automatic zero for your final project. 

## Final Deliverables

For the project submission, you should submit the following by the due date above:

* The Visualizations
    * Depending on the software you choice, submit the workbook that contains your visualizations.

    * A citation of the dataset. 
        * Where you found it (URL)
        * Author
        * Last Updated Date (If available)
    * Short Description of the dataset in your own words.
    * Explanations of what your visualizations are representing.
