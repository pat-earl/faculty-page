## Graphical Excellence

* Edward Tufte describes *Excellence* in visualization as presenting 
complex ideas with clarity, precision, and efficiency.
* Graphical displays should:
    * *Show the data.*
    * Have the viewer think about the display rather than how it was created.
    * Avoid distorting the data.
    * Presentation of many numbers in a small space.
    * Make large data sets manageable.

---

* (continued)
  * encourage the eye to compare.
  * reveal the data at several levels of detail.
  * serve a reasonably clear purpose: description, exploration, tabulation, or decoration.
  * closely integrated with the statistical and verbal descriptions of a dataset.

---

## Best Practices?
* "Beauty is in the eye of the beholder"
* Some guiding principles:
  * Define a clear purpose.
  * Know the audience.
  * Use visual features to show the data properly.
    * (Using the correct chart type)
  * Keep it organized and coherent.
  * Keep it inclusive.
  * Don't distort the data.
