## Requirements

* Unless otherwise stated in the handout, programming assignment submissions
that don't compile will receive a zero (0).
* Project Plans are required for the first two programs and program 4. You can 
download the template [here](./project_plan.docx)
* All assignments are due by 11:59PM of the due date. Late submissions will be 
accepted for up to two (2) days, with a 10% penalty applied for each day. Any 
submissions received beyond that will not be accepted.
