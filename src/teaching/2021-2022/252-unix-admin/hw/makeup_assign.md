## Description

Research and write a paper about one of the following *Linux* compression/archiving tools 
(and related unarchive tool) from the list below.

1. bzip2 (bzcat & bunzip2)
2. gzip (zcat & gunzip)
3. tar
4. zip (unzip)

Your paper should include the following:

- A brief history of the command (i.e: Who made it, why was it created, etc.).
- The compression algorithm(s) the command uses.
- How to run the command without any options.
- At least two command options you find interesting and what they do.

Your paper shouldn't be longer than a page in length. Be sure to include your name, professor's name, course, and
date at the top of the paper. Submit your paper in Word DOCX or PDF format in the "Class Make-up Assignment" on D2L.
