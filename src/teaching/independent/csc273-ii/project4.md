## Description

As a new system analyst for the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, you are tasked
to assist management with budget forecasting. You are to acquire the 2014 through 2019 
home-purchasing summary data from six government and private sources. You have performed your vendor 
management due diligence activities in acquiring the six databases (System A through F.) Your next 
task is to study the six databases (data, meta data and formats) and integrate them into one 
database, which will be used to import into the HUD ERP system. The source databases being Project 3 
Systems Data.xlsx. The target database is the HUD Centralized System.xlsx. Note: For this exercise 
you will only collect one year per database. System A through E correlated to the year 2019 to 2014 
respectively (e.g. System A contains 2019 data, System B contains 2018, etc.) All data are AVERAGE 
type of data, including the U.S. summary data. Mark “N/A” if no data.
